---
name: Narrative overlay rule
description: Persistent UX guidance for FLIKKER's story and character messages.
---

Narrative messages must remain outside the active play path. Use a compact upper rail with explicit dismissal and a short automatic timeout; never center a large dialogue card over the player, platforms, or combat.

**Why:** Center-stage cards obscured the action and made the platforming experience feel interrupted rather than atmospheric.

**How to apply:** Keep story beats and character encounters stacked in the upper rail, preserve pointer access for the close control, and verify the layout on both desktop and narrow touch screens.
